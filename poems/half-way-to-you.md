commit_message:: "Halfway to You: ten years of Blonde, (nearly) twenty weeks of becoming
A song for our baby at twenty weeks, written on the tenth anniversary of Frank Ocean's Blonde—woven through with references to every song on 
the album that binds Abi and me together in memory and music. The same songs us and our three grew up listening to, back when we were all ten years 
younger. Now Jada is 24, Tiana moving to London at 22, Junior becoming a man at 17. Abi at 34, me at 42. Older, tired, unprepared, exhausted—
but full of love and so proud of everything we've built and are building together.
Pink and blue. No name yet. Just halfway, and already changed by you."

**Verse 1**

A record turned ten this morning,
Jada turned twenty-four before.
Tiana’s boxes fill the hallway,
Junior doesn’t need a lift anymore.
Three pairs of shoes *(Nikes)* are walking onwards,
three childhoods passing through the door;
and underneath those same old songs
another heartbeat asks for more.

Your mother wore our eldest’s age
when that hidden cover first appeared.
She thought that she were grown back then—
we're only younger than our fear.
Now ivy climbs around the memories,
ten summers flower out of view;
a white car moves across the years,
carrying who we were into view.
We put the old nights on again
and hear them differently through you.

**Pre-chorus**

I *(solo)* thought by now I’d know the road,
have every answer ready too.
But I have never been your father.
I have never travelled here to you.

**Chorus**

We’re halfway to you,
halfway to your first morning,
halfway through the quiet dark
where all your life is forming.

No name yet for the colour,
no need to choose between the two—
there’s pink and white, there’s pink and blue,
and every shade belongs to you.

We’re halfway to holding you,
but wholly held by what you do.
So close to her, so close to me—
we’re already changed by you.

**Verse 2**

Some nights I feel all forty-two years
settling heavy in my bones.
I have climbed this mountain three times,
but each child makes the climb their own.
I know the weight behind the wonder,
the promises a good man keeps;
and sometimes all my self-control
gives way while everybody sleeps.

I have less of that old certainty,
less fire to waste and more to lose;
but I know now that love is something
we are called each day to choose.
I know the ordinary mornings
are the ones that disappear;
so I will try to meet each moment
while the moment is still here.

**Pre-chorus**

Perhaps the fear is not a warning *(I’m not brave).*
Perhaps it’s love becoming true.
Making doors inside my doubt
and opening every one to you.

**Chorus**

We’re halfway to you,
halfway to your first morning,
halfway through the quiet dark
where all your life is forming.

No name yet for the colour,
no need to choose between the two—
there’s pink and white, there’s pink and blue,
and every shade belongs to you.

We’re halfway to holding you,
but wholly held by what you do.
So close to her, so close to me—
we’re already changed by you.

**Bridge**

Be yourself, whoever that is *(I’d rather live outside).*
You owe no version to our dreams.
Son or daughter, both or neither,
you are more than what a colour means.

May you know the ones before you
in the stories, songs and names;
in island sun and English rainfall,
in all the blood from which you came.

Godspeed to those whose lives made ours,
whose faces time has taken from view.
Godspeed to the ones still here,
making room and waiting too.

The doors are open, lights are on;
the table is prepared for you.
A place among the names before us,
and all the names that follow you.

Godspeed to every life beyond us,
to the branches we may never see—
the ancestors that we follow,
and the ancestors we will be.

May love run in both directions
through every unknown name and face:
backwards into those who made us,
forwards into those you’ll make.

**Final chorus**

We’re halfway to you,
but you’ve come so far already—
through every life that led to ours,
through your mother, strong and steady.

The skyline’s waiting past the window.
The future’s open, bright and new.
We won’t decide the life before you;
we’ll make a space and walk with you.

We’re halfway to holding you,
but wholly held by what you do.
So close to her, so close to me—
we’re already home to you.

**Outro**

Three children walking towards their lives,
one child travelling into view.
A record turns. The years move on.

And we move halfway—
halfway—
always halfway to you.
