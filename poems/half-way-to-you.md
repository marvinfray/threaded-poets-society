# Halfway to You

**Verse 1**

A record turned ten this morning,  
one turned twenty-four before.  
Boxes fill the hallway now,  
the youngest doesn't need a lift anymore.  

Six [Air Forces](https://open.spotify.com/track/19YKaevk2bce4odJkP5L22) walking onwards,  
three childhoods passing through the door;  
and underneath those same old songs  
another heartbeat asks for more.  

Your mother wore our eldest’s age  
when that hidden cover first appeared.  
She thought that we were grown back then—  
we were only younger than our grief, our fear.  

**Pre-chorus**

Now [ivy](https://open.spotify.com/track/2ZWlPOoWh0626oTaHrnl2a) climbs around the memories,  
ten summers fading out of view;  
[old stories flicker on our screens](https://open.spotify.com/track/73D5hBkiVZkopZrmBZTBWf),  
a cropped and carefully chosen view.  
The pictures kept our younger faces;  
we kept the life they never knew.  
A [white car](https://open.spotify.com/track/2LMkwUfqC6S6s6qDVlEuzV) moves across the years,  
carrying all we lived to you.  
We put the old [nights](https://open.spotify.com/track/4Jle0Cjj88YkM7jbAjiFjf) on again  
and hear them differently with you.  

**Chorus**

We’re halfway to you,  
halfway to your first morning,  
halfway beneath your mother’s heart,  
where all your life is forming.  

No name yet for the colour,  
no need to choose between the two—  
there’s [pink and white](https://open.spotify.com/track/3xKsf9qdS1CyvXSMEid6g8), there’s pink and blue,  
and every shade belongs to you.  

We’re halfway to holding you,  
though you already hold us too.  
So [close to her, so close to me](https://open.spotify.com/track/1VZLEW5ZfcAbKZ94XQiSZF)—  
we’re already changed by you.  

**Verse 2**

Some nights I feel all forty-two years  
settling [so low](https://open.spotify.com/track/35xSkNIXi504fcEwz9USRB) in my bones.  
I have climbed this mountain three times, (and buried hopes before).  
but each child makes the climb their own.  

I know the weight behind the wonder,  
the promises a [good man](https://open.spotify.com/track/2JUqYobT8NvARdPmc4ES2x) lives to keep;  
and sometimes all my [self-control](https://open.spotify.com/track/5GUYJTQap5F3RDQiCOJhrS)   
gives way while everybody sleeps.  

Perhaps the fear is not a warning *([I’m not brave](https://open.spotify.com/track/1BViPjTT585XAhkUUrkts0)))*  
Perhaps it’s love becoming true:  
making doors inside my doubt 
and opening every one to you.  

**Chorus**

We’re halfway to you,  
halfway to your first morning,  
halfway beneath your mother’s heart,  
where all your life is forming.  

No name yet for the colour,  
no need to choose between the two—  
there’s [pink and white](https://open.spotify.com/track/3xKsf9qdS1CyvXSMEid6g8), there’s pink and blue,  
and every shade belongs to you.  

We’re halfway to holding you,  
though you already hold us too.  
So [close to her, so close to me](https://open.spotify.com/track/1VZLEW5ZfcAbKZ94XQiSZF)—  
we’re already changed by you.  

**Bridge** 

[Be yourself](https://open.spotify.com/track/1IdZvb2XK8NmsovQlV6CPn), whoever that is 
*([I’d rather live outside](https://open.spotify.com/track/1BViPjTT585XAhkUUrkts0)).*  
You own a version of our dreams  
*([dreaming of a thought](https://open.spotify.com/track/1BViPjTT585XAhkUUrkts0)).* 
May you know the ones before you  
in the stories, songs and names;  
in island sun and English rainfall,  
in all the blood from which you came.  

[Godspeed](https://open.spotify.com/track/34xTFwjPQ1dC6uJmleno7x) to those whose lives made ours,  
and the ones still making room.  

The doors are open, lights are on;  
[the table is prepared for you](https://open.spotify.com/track/34xTFwjPQ1dC6uJmleno7x).  
A place among the names before us,  
and all the names that follow you.  

[Godspeed](https://open.spotify.com/track/34xTFwjPQ1dC6uJmleno7x) to every life beyond us,  
to the branches we may never see—  
the ancestors that we follow,  
and the ancestors we will be.  

May love run in both directions  
through every unknown name and face:  
backwards into those who made us,  
forwards into those you’ll make.  

**Final chorus** 

We’re halfway to you,  
but you’ve come so far already—  
through every life that led to ours,  
through your mother, strong and steady.  

The [skyline’s](https://open.spotify.com/track/4xR3MAscflQ262kMeiKshQ) waiting past the window.  
The [future’s](https://open.spotify.com/track/5k8LB57xOq8UUNVaKWSqrf) open, bright and new.  
We won’t decide the life before you;  
we’ll make a space and walk with you.  

We’re halfway to holding you,  
though you already hold us too.  
So [close to her, so close to me](https://open.spotify.com/track/1VZLEW5ZfcAbKZ94XQiSZF)—  
we’re already home to you.  

**Outro**  

Walk towards your lives.  
Travel into view.  
The record turns; the years [reprise](https://open.spotify.com/track/2qtoRFCOEL1gRn5q9DJC7F)  
the younger selves we thought we knew.  

We know you’ll be [pretty sweet](https://open.spotify.com/track/17yrCsl1Ai6CZLBmGj6d6p),  
whatever sweetness means to you.  
Your future’s yours, [futura free](https://open.spotify.com/track/5k8LB57xOq8UUNVaKWSqrf)—  
we're meeting you here.
