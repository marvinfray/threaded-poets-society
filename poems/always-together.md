  For our little one, not yet here. Sung to the tune of Twinkle Twinkle

commit: |-
  I wrote this one for our unborn child. I wanted something we could sing
  to our bump, and share with our closest friends (and baby cousins). I
  borrowed the most obvious lullaby tune I know and filled it with a promise.
  It reflects conversations between us about how we perhaps romanticise
  parenthood, but also hold a deeply spiritual, magical sense of it: that
  we'll be together through everything — the weather, the seasons, the
  years, the miles, and even past my own days. I ended it in the starry sky
  so the song comes home to the melody it started with.
---

When the rain clouds gather grey,  
When the wind blows hard all day,  
When the sun comes shining through,  
I'll be right there next to you.  
*I'll be with you, you with me,*  
*Close as close as we can be.*  

Spring will come and summer too,  
Every season, I'll love you.  
Autumn leaves and winter snow,  
Every year I'll watch you grow.  
*I'll be with you, you with me,*  
*Close as close as we can be.*  

When you're small enough to hold,  
When you're grown and I am old,  
Every candle, every year,  
I will always hold you dear.  
*I'll be with you, you with me,*  
*Close as close as we can be.*  

If one day you're far away,  
Over mountains, 'cross the bay,  
Look up at the moon tonight,  
I'll be seeing that same light.  
*I'll be with you, you with me,*  
*Close as close as we can be.*  

When you wake to greet the day,  
When you laugh and when you play,  
When you close your eyes to sleep,  
You're a love I'll always keep.  
*I'll be with you, you with me,*  
*Close as close as we can be.*  

Even when my days are through,  
There'll be part of me in you.  
Listen when the breezes sigh,  
Find me in the starry sky.  
*I'll be with you, you with me,*  
*Close as close as we can be.*
